Roasted Butternut Squash Soup

A rich, creamy, dairy-free variation of the Love & Lemons soup, with caramelized squash and fresh herbs.

Total Time
1 hr
Servings
6
Category
import on 2026-11-11 16:01:40

Ingredients

  • 1 medium-large butternut squash (about 3 lb whole), peeled, seeded, and cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 2 tablespoons extra-virgin olive oil, divided
  • 1 large yellow onion, chopped
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt, plus more to taste
  • 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 1 tablespoon chopped fresh sage
  • 1/2 tablespoon minced fresh rosemary
  • 1 teaspoon grated fresh ginger
  • 2 1/2 to 4 cups vegetable broth, as needed
  • Freshly ground black pepper
  • Optional finish: 1 to 2 teaspoons apple cider vinegar or fresh lemon juice
  • Optional serving: chopped parsley, toasted pepitas, crusty bread

Instructions

  1. Roast the squash. Heat oven to 425°F. Toss the squash cubes with 1 tablespoon olive oil and a little salt and pepper. Spread in one layer on a large baking sheet. Roast 30 to 40 minutes, turning halfway through, until soft and browned at the edges.
  2. Cook the onion. While the squash roasts, warm the remaining 1 tablespoon olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and 1/2 teaspoon salt. Cook 8 to 10 minutes, stirring occasionally, until soft and lightly golden.
  3. Bloom the aromatics. Add the garlic, sage, rosemary, and ginger. Stir for about 1 minute, just until fragrant.
  4. Simmer. Add the roasted squash and 2 1/2 cups vegetable broth. Bring to a gentle simmer and cook 5 to 10 minutes to bring the flavors together.
  5. Blend until creamy. Let cool slightly. Transfer to a Vitamix or other blender in batches if necessary. Blend until completely smooth. Add extra broth a little at a time to reach your preferred thickness. Use care when blending hot soup.
  6. Season and serve. Taste and adjust salt and pepper. For extra brightness, stir in 1 to 2 teaspoons apple cider vinegar or lemon juice. Serve hot with parsley, pepitas, and crusty bread, if desired.
