Pasta Bolognese with Garlic Ricotta

Total Time
35 mins
Servings
4 Servings
Category
import on 2026-11-11 16:01:40

Ingredients

  • 18 oz Ground Beef
  • 1 14.5-oz can Crushed Tomatoes
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 1 oz Pickled Goathorn Peppers
  • 1/2 cup Part-Skim Ricotta Cheese
  • 3/4 lb Carrots
  • 2 Tbsps Vegetarian Worcestershire Sauce
  • 1/4 cup Cream
  • 3/4 lb Elicoidali Pasta
  • 1 Sweet Onion
  • 2 Tbsps Mascarpone Cheese

Instructions

  1. Fill a large pot 3/4 of the way up with salted water; cover and heat to boiling on high.
  2. Wash, dry, and peel the carrots; halve lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise.
  3. Halve, peel, and small dice the onion.
  4. Roughly chop the peppers.
  5. Peel 1 clove of garlic; using a zester or the small side of a box grater, finely grate into a paste.
  6. In a bowl, combine the ricotta, 1 tablespoon of olive oil, and as much of the garlic paste as you'd like. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. In a large pan, heat a drizzle of olive oil on medium-high until hot.
  8. Add the beef and sliced carrots; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring frequently and breaking the meat apart with a spoon, 4 to 5 minutes, or until lightly browned.
  9. Add the diced onion; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, 3 to 4 minutes, or until slightly softened and the beef is browned and cooked through.
  10. Add the chopped peppers. Cook, stirring frequently, 1 to 2 minutes, or until combined.
  11. Add the tomatoes (carefully, as the liquid may splatter), cream, and worcestershire sauce; season with salt and pepper. Cook, stirring occasionally, 2 to 3 minutes, or until the sauce is slightly thickened.
  12. Turn off the heat. Taste, then season with salt and pepper if desired.
  13. Meanwhile, add the pasta to the pot of boiling water. Cook 10 to 12 minutes, or until al dente (still slightly firm to the bite). Turn off the heat.
  14. Reserving 1/2 cup of the pasta cooking water, drain thoroughly and return to the pot.
  15. To the pot of cooked pasta, add the bolognese and half the reserved pasta cooking water. Cook on medium-high, stirring frequently, 1 to 2 minutes, or until coated (if necessary, gradually add the remaining cooking water to ensure the pasta is thoroughly coated).
  16. Turn off the heat. Stir in the mascarpone until combined. Taste, then season with salt and pepper if desired.
  17. Serve the finished pasta topped with the garlic ricotta. Enjoy!
