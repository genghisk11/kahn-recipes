Sticky Honey-Garlic Glazed Chicken

Sweet, savory, tangy and caramelized

Total Time
45 mins
Servings
Serves 4
Category
import on 2026-11-11 16:08:43

Ingredients

  • [Chicken]
  • 1½–2 lb boneless, skinless chicken thighs (or breasts)
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp kosher salt
  • ¼ tsp black pepper
  • [Sticky glaze]
  • 3 tbsp honey
  • 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 tbsp apple cider vinegar
  • 2 garlic cloves, finely minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp brown sugar (optional)
  • ½ tsp grated fresh ginger (optional)

Instructions

  1. 1. Season. Preheat oven to 400°F. Pat chicken dry. Rub with olive oil, garlic powder, paprika, salt and pepper. Arrange in a lightly oiled baking dish.
  2. 2. Mix the glaze. Whisk honey, soy sauce, Dijon, vinegar, garlic, olive oil, brown sugar and ginger until smooth. Reserve about one-third in a separate clean bowl.
  3. 3. Bake and reglaze. Brush chicken with the other two-thirds of the glaze. Bake uncovered for 20 minutes, then brush with the reserved glaze. Continue baking 5–15 minutes, depending on thickness, until the thickest part reaches 165°F.
  4. 4. Caramelize. Broil for 1–2 minutes, watching closely so the honey does not burn. Rest 5 minutes before serving.
