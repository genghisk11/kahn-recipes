Recipe for Asian-Style Sautéed Aromatics

Category
import on 2026-11-11 16:01:40

Ingredients

  • 3 Tbsps finely chopped peeled ginger, about one 3" piece
  • 3 Tbsps finely chopped scallions
  • 1 Tbsp finely chopped garlic
  • 3 Tbsps neutral oil

Instructions

  1. Prep and finely chop the ginger, scallions, and garlic. Use both the white and the green portions of the scallions.
  2. In a small sauce pot, heat the oil. Add the chopped aromatics, season with salt and pepper, and cook for 5-7 minutes on low heat, stirring occasionally, until the aromatics are softened.
