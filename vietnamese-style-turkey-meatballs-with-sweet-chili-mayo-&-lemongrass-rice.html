Vietnamese-Style Turkey Meatballs with Sweet Chili Mayo & Lemongrass Rice

Total Time
45 mins
Servings
2 servings
Category
import on 2026-11-11 16:01:40

Ingredients

  • 18 oz Ground Turkey
  • 1/2 cup Panko Breadcrumbs
  • 1 cup Long Grain White Rice
  • 1 stalk Lemongrass
  • 3/4 lb Carrots
  • 1 Jalapeño Pepper
  • 1/3 cup East Asian-Style Sautéed Aromatics
  • 4 Persian Cucumbers
  • 1/4 cup Sweet Chili Sauce
  • 2 Tbsps Rice Vinegar
  • 1/4 cup Mayonnaise
  • 1 Tbsp Sugar
  • 3 Tbsps Roasted Peanuts

Instructions

  1. Place an oven rack in the center of the oven; preheat to 450°F.
  2. Wash and dry the fresh produce.
  3. Using the back (blunt edge) of your knife, hit along the length of the lemongrass to release the oils. Cut into 2-inch pieces.
  4. Peel the carrots and grate on the large side of a box grater.
  5. Halve the cucumbers lengthwise, then thinly slice crosswise.
  6. Roughly chop the peanuts.
  7. Cut off and discard the stem of the pepper. Halve lengthwise; remove the ribs and seeds, then thinly slice crosswise. Thoroughly wash your hands immediately after handling.
  8. In a bowl, combine the mayonnaise and sweet chili sauce.
  9. Carefully rinse the rice (sifting through for any impurities). Drain thoroughly.
  10. In a medium pot, combine the rice, lemongrass pieces, a big pinch of salt, and 2 cups of water. Heat to boiling on high.
  11. Once boiling, reduce the heat to low. Cover and cook, without stirring, 13 to 15 minutes, or until the water has been absorbed and the rice is tender.
  12. Turn off the heat. Carefully discard the lemongrass pieces, then fluff the cooked rice with a fork.
  13. Meanwhile, line a sheet pan with foil.
  14. In a bowl, combine the turkey, breadcrumbs, and sautéed aromatics. Season with salt and pepper; gently mix to combine.
  15. Shape the mixture into 16 tightly packed meatballs.
  16. Transfer to the sheet pan; arrange in an even layer.
  17. Bake 13 to 15 minutes, or until browned and cooked through.
  18. Remove from the oven.
  19. Meanwhile, in a medium bowl, combine the sugar and vinegar; season with salt and pepper. Whisk until the sugar has dissolved.
  20. Add the grated carrots, sliced cucumbers, and as much of the sliced pepper as you'd like, depending on how spicy you'd like the dish to be. Set aside to marinate, stirring occasionally, at least 10 minutes. Taste, then season with salt and pepper if desired.
  21. Serve the lemongrass rice topped with the baked meatballs and marinated vegetables. Drizzle with the sweet chili mayo. Garnish with the chopped peanuts. Enjoy!
