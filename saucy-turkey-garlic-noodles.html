Saucy Turkey Garlic Noodles

A kid-friendly noodle dish with crispy ground turkey and a sweet-savory garlic sauce.

Category
import on 2026-11-11 16:01:40

Ingredients

  • [Main]
  • 1 lb ground turkey
  • 8 oz noodles (ramen, lo mein, spaghetti, or udon)
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • [Sauce]
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp brown sugar or honey
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar or lime juice
  • 1 tsp sesame oil (optional)
  • [Optional Add-ins]
  • Broccoli
  • Frozen peas
  • Corn
  • Shredded carrots
  • [Toppings]
  • Green onions
  • Sesame seeds
  • Crushed peanuts or crispy onions

Instructions

  1. [Cook the noodles]
  2. Cook according to package directions. Drain and set aside.
  3. [Brown the turkey]
  4. Heat olive oil in a large skillet. Cook turkey over medium-high heat until browned and slightly crispy, about 7–8 minutes. Season lightly with salt and pepper.
  5. [Make the sauce]
  6. Mix soy sauce, brown sugar, vinegar or lime juice, and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  7. [Garlic + butter]
  8. Push turkey to one side of the pan. Add butter and garlic to the empty side and cook about 30 seconds until fragrant.
  9. [Combine]
  10. Add noodles and sauce to the pan. Toss until glossy and coated. Stir in vegetables during the last few minutes if using.
