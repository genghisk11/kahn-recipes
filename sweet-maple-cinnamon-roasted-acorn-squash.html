Sweet Maple-Cinnamon Roasted Acorn Squash

Butter-free | Oven: 350°F | Serves 2–4 | Prep: 10 min | Roast: 45–55 min

Prep Time
10 mins
Total Time
55 mins
Servings
Serves 2–4

Ingredients

  • 1 medium acorn squash
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil (or melted coconut oil)
  • 2 tablespoons pure maple syrup
  • 1–2 tablespoons brown sugar, to taste
  • ½ teaspoon ground cinnamon
  • Pinch of ground nutmeg (optional)
  • ¼ teaspoon salt
  • Optional: toasted pecans or walnuts for serving

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper.
  2. Carefully halve the acorn squash and scoop out the seeds. Cut into wedges about 1–2 inches thick.
  3. Whisk olive oil, maple syrup, brown sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg (if using), and salt together in a small bowl.
  4. Arrange wedges in a single layer on the baking sheet. Brush both cut sides with the glaze, reserving a little for later.
  5. Roast for 45–55 minutes, turning the wedges halfway through. Brush with the reserved glaze during the last 10 minutes.
  6. The squash is ready when a fork slides easily into the flesh and the edges are golden. Serve warm, with toasted nuts if desired.
